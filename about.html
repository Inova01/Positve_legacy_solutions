Our Story

Built to Serve Our Community

Positive Legacy Solutions LLC was founded on a simple idea: families deserve one trusted place for the services that shape their future. Founder and CEO Indigne Etienne brought insurance, real estate, tax and immigration guidance together under one roof in Jacksonville, Florida.

Serving the community since 1995, Indigne brings experience as a licensed insurance agent, licensed Realtor and accounting graduate. He built Positive Legacy Solutions on three pillars: honesty, reliability and compliance.

We speak your language — English, Français and Kreyòl — and we treat every client like family, because building your legacy is personal to us.

Our Mission

To simplify life's biggest decisions for our community by delivering trusted, bilingual guidance across insurance, real estate, tax and immigration — all in one caring relationship.

Our Vision

To be Jacksonville's most trusted multi-service partner — the first name families think of when they're ready to protect, grow and secure their legacy.
